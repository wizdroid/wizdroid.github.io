1. Overview

Through naturalization (Einbürgerung), you acquire German citizenship with all associated rights and obligations. This guide provides a complete overview of the naturalization application process for the city of Erfurt, Germany.

📌 What You'll Get

German citizenship grants you the right to live, work, and study anywhere in Germany and the European Union. You gain access to social benefits, consular protection abroad, and the right to vote in federal elections.

This guide is based on the official application package from the Standesamt (Civil Registry Office) of Erfurt.

2. General Requirements

Before applying, verify that you meet the basic eligibility criteria. The Federal Commissioner for Migration, Refugees and Integration provides comprehensive information at www.einbuergerung.de.

Legal Residence

5-8 years in Germany, or 7 years with integration course

German Language

B1 level or higher (Goethe-Zertifikat, TestDaF, DSH)

Clean Criminal Record

No serious offenses in Germany or abroad

Financial Self-Sufficiency

No reliance on social benefits (SGB II/XII)

Knowledge of Germany

Pass the "Leben in Deutschland" naturalization test

Renunciation

Give up previous citizenship (some exceptions apply)

3. Application Process

1

Online Check or Counseling Appointment

Option A: Use the Einbürgerungs-Check on the Erfurt website for initial assessment. If eligible, download and print application forms.

Option B (Recommended): Schedule a counseling appointment with the Standesamt. They provide personalized guidance and a tailored document list.

Email: einbuergerung@erfurt.de

Note: Due to high demand, there may be longer wait times for appointments.
2

Submit Your Application

  • Each person aged 16+ must submit their own application
  • Minor children under 16 can be naturalized with one parent
  • Complete all fields in print letters
  • Mark N/A fields as "entfällt" or "nicht zutreffend"

Submission Methods:

  • Mail: Send signed application with copies to the address below
  • Drop Box: Place in sealed envelope in Bürgeramt mailbox at Bürgermeister-Wagner-Straße 1
3

Application Review

After submitting complete documents, your application will be examined. The naturalization authority will verify all information and may request additional documents.

4

Decision and Fee Payment

Upon approval, you will be notified and required to pay the naturalization fee. After payment, you receive your certificate of naturalization.

5. Application Form Guide

The application form consists of 12 sections. Here's what each section requires:

1 Personal Details

Family name, first name(s), previous names, birth name, date/place of birth, current address, contact details (phone, email - voluntary), marital status.

2 Spouse/Partner Details

Complete if married or in a registered partnership: partner's full name, birth name, date/place of birth, address, nationality, and whether partner is also applying.

3 Status, Military Service, Nationality

  • 3.1 Nationality: Current nationalities, how acquired, previous nationalities, how lost
  • 3.2 Special Status: Asylum seeker, refugee, stateless person (if applicable)
  • 3.3 Military Service: Are you subject to/-exempted from/have you performed military service?

4 Parents

Biological or adoptive parents, parents' marriage details, each parent's name, birth name, date/place of birth, nationality, ethnicity, address or date of death.

5 Children

List all children (minor and adult): full name, birth name, date/place of birth, nationality, whether child should also be naturalized.

6 Residence, Education, Employment

  • 6.1 Foreign Residence: All stays outside Germany since birth
  • 6.2 Germany Residence: All stays in Germany, current permit type and expiry
  • 6.3 Education: School, vocational training, university
  • 6.4 Employment: Last 8 years of work history

7 Language, Integration, Criminal Record, Financial

  • 7.1 German Language: How learned, B1/B2 certificate, naturalization test
  • 7.2 Criminal Record: Any offenses, fines over €500, investigations
  • 7.3 Financial Self-Sufficiency: Occupation, income, assets, debts, benefits received
  • 7.4-7.5: Family income and maintenance details

8 Previous Applications

Have you previously applied for naturalization in Germany? If yes, which authority and outcome.

9 Reason for Naturalization

Explain why you are applying for German citizenship.

10 Photo

Attach a passport photo (required for applicants 16+).

11 Declarations and Consents

Critical section with accuracy declaration, data protection notice, fee information, consent to inquiries with various authorities, and tax confidentiality waiver. Signature and date required.

12 Naturalization of Children

If applying with children, list them here. Legal guardians must sign.

7. Important Declarations

Reporting Obligations During Processing

You must immediately inform the naturalization authority of any changes:

⚠️ Critical: Report All Changes

Failure to report changes can result in rejection or revocation of naturalization.

Address Changes

  • Moving
  • Street name changes
  • Secondary residence
  • Incarceration
  • Abroad stay exceeding 6 months

Family Status Changes

  • Marriage, registered partnership
  • Separation, divorce, dissolution
  • Death of spouse/partner
  • Birth/death of child
  • Adoption, name changes

Income Changes

  • Start/stop of social benefits (SGB II, SGB XII)
  • Unemployment, short-time work
  • Salary changes, self-employment income
  • Housing benefit, child benefit, parental allowance

Other Changes to Report

  • Employment changes (job change, termination, new self-employment)
  • Asset changes (property purchase/sale)
  • Insurance changes
  • Retirement provision changes
  • Residence permit changes
  • Travel document changes
  • Acquisition of additional nationalities
  • Criminal investigations

Consequences of False Information

  • Revocation: Naturalization can be revoked if obtained through false information (§ 35 StAG)
  • Criminal Penalties: Up to 5 years imprisonment or fine for fraud (§ 42 StAG)
  • 10-Year Bar: If revoked due to fraud, cannot apply again for 10 years (§ 35a StAG)
